### Improvement Analysis

Based on the week's performance, three specific adjustments are proposed for next week:

**1. Entry patience — avoid chasing into prior-month resistance**
MSFT and AMZN were both entered at or near prior-high levels without confirmed breakout momentum. NVDA and AAPL, by contrast, were entered at pullback support (NVDA) or confirmed new-high breakout (AAPL) — both outperformed. Pattern: entries on pullbacks to support or confirmed breakouts to new highs outperform entries at resistance levels. Next week, for any symbol approaching but not breaking prior resistance, wait for a confirmed close through resistance before entering, or size down to half position and add on confirmation.

**2. Add weight to post-entry momentum as a HOLD/ADD signal**
During midday scans, NVDA's exceptional volume (188M shares on May 7) was noted and treated only as a "thesis confirming" observation. It could have been a signal to raise the stop sooner rather than waiting for the next session. Similarly, AAPL's new 52-week high on entry day could prompt a next-session re-evaluation for a potential add. Next week: establish an explicit rule — if a position gains >3% on above-average volume on any single day, assess stop raise and partial-add opportunity at midday rather than waiting for the next pre-market session.

**3. Guardrails volume check — prioritize the data fix**
The V situation represents the most concrete operational improvement available. The guardrails.py volume check should be updated to query Perplexity for ADV when IEX volume is below the minimum threshold, cross-referencing before blocking a trade on a known large-cap. Until the fix is implemented, any guardrail volume block on a symbol where Perplexity has confirmed high liquidity should be flagged explicitly in the journal with a recommendation to the user to review.

No changes to hard risk rules (position size limits, stop-loss floors, cash reserve, sector concentration) are proposed — these are functioning correctly and protecting capital.
